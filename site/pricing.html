FAQ

Questions owners often ask.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You tell me about your business in your own words. I take care of the rest and explain anything you need to know in plain language.

What do I need to send you?

Your business name, address, opening hours, what you offer and how people can contact you. A logo and a few photos help, but they are not required to get started.

I don't have good photos. Is that a problem?

No. Photos taken on a recent phone in good daylight usually work well. I can tell you what to shoot, and the design works with just a few pictures.

I already have a website, but it is broken or out of date.

That is very common. Send me the link and I will tell you honestly whether it makes more sense to fix it or start fresh.

Do I pay before I see anything?

No. I send you a preview of your website first, to look at on your own phone. You pay only when you approve it. The price is agreed in writing before any work starts.

How long does it take?

It depends mostly on how quickly you can send the basics. A simple one-page website is a small project, so it is a matter of days, not months. I will give you a date before we start.

Who owns my website and my domain?

You do. Your domain is registered in your own name and account, and I help you set it up. The website files are yours too: you can ask me for a copy at any time.

What happens after my website is live?

Hosting is included at no extra cost, and there is no monthly fee to me. For 30 days after launch, small fixes are included, like a typo or a wrong opening hour. After that, any change is quoted separately before I do anything.

Will you still be around later?

You can reach me at the same email and number. And you are never stuck: your domain is in your name and you can have the website files at any time, so anyone else can take over if you ever need them to.

Why is it more affordable than an agency?

Because the websites are simple on purpose: one clear page with what local customers actually need, instead of a large custom project. And while the studio is new, the first websites come at a launch price.
