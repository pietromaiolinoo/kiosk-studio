How it works

Three steps. No tech talk.

You pay only when you have seen and approved your website. The price is agreed in writing before any work starts.

Pricing & FAQ →
  1. 01

    You send the basics

    Your name, logo if you have one, a few photos, opening hours and what you offer. A short message or a quick call is enough.

  2. 02

    You see a preview

    I build your site and send you a link to look at on your phone. You tell me what to change, in plain words. You pay only when you approve it.

  3. 03

    It goes live

    Your website goes online at your own address, with hosting included. For 30 days after launch, small fixes are on me.
