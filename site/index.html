FAQ

Questions owners often ask.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You tell me about your business in your own words. I take care of the rest and explain anything you need to know in plain language.

What do I need to send you?

Your business name, address, opening hours, what you offer and how people can contact you. A logo and a few photos help, but they are not required to get started.

I don't have good photos. Is that a problem?

No. Photos taken on a recent phone in good daylight usually work well. I can tell you what to shoot, and the design works with just a few pictures.

I already have a website, but it is broken or out of date.

That is very common. Send me the link and I will tell you honestly whether it makes more sense to fix it or start fresh.

How long does it take?

It depends mostly on how quickly you can send the basics. A simple one-page website is a small project, so it is a matter of days, not months. I will give you a date before we start.

Can I change things later?

Yes. With the care plan, small updates like new hours or prices are included. Without it, just ask and I will tell you what a change costs before doing it.

Why is it more affordable than an agency?

Because the websites are simple on purpose. I focus on what local customers actually need and reuse designs I have refined for each type of business, instead of starting from zero every time.
